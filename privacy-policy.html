Privacy Policy

Trendy Education Service LLP · Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Trendy Education Service LLP (“Trendy Traders Academy”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website, enquire about our courses or enrol in a programme.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry details: when you request a consultation, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, age, the amount you plan to trade with, your occupation, your WhatsApp brochure preference and your email marketing choice.
  • Enrolment and payment details: information needed to enrol you and process payments. Card and bank details are handled by our payment providers.
  • Usage data: pages visited, links clicked and similar technical information, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and help you choose a suitable course.
  • To confirm that our programmes are offered only to adults aged 18 and above.
  • To deliver courses, manage enrolments and process payments.
  • To send the course brochure on WhatsApp, only if you choose “Yes”.
  • To send promotional emails about batches and resources, only if you tick the optional marketing box. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it, and to meet legal obligations.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example payment, email, customer relationship management and analytics providers), when required by law, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

Analytics and cookies

We use analytics tools to measure website usage. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or other enquiry details to analytics platforms. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent for marketing at any time, by emailing update@trendytraders.in. We handle requests in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as its provisions apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
